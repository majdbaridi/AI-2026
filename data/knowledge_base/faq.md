Bicycles are permitted on many regional and suburban trains. A separate bicycle ticket may be required, while advance reservations are generally not available on regional services.

On selected long-distance trains, bicycles can be transported in designated spaces. Passengers usually need both a bicycle ticket and a confirmed reservation.

Passengers may be entitled to compensation when a long-distance train is significantly delayed. A delay of 60 minutes may qualify for a 25 percent refund, while delays of 120 minutes or more may qualify for a 50 percent refund.

Seat reservations are usually optional but are recommended on busy long-distance routes. Reservations for bicycle spaces are typically mandatory where bicycle transport is offered.

Tickets can normally be purchased through the operator’s mobile app or website, at ticket machines, and at service centres. Digital services may also provide live departure times, delay information, and platform updates.
